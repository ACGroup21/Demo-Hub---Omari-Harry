The funding maths, checked against gov.uk’s own worked examples

Every figure in the Sandbox walkthrough comes out of one calculation core. This page runs that core against 49 published worked examples — the levy formula, the August 2026 top-up boundary, co-investment and its age waivers, funding-band drawdown, FIFO expiry, the incentive and hiring payments, and timezone-safe date handling.

It runs now, in your browser. Nothing here is a screenshot of a result: each row calls the function and prints what came back beside what gov.uk says it should be. Each case links the rule it rests on.

Running…
What it testsThe calculation core itself — the module with no interface attached — not a re-implementation written to agree with it.
Where the rules come fromA governed rulebook loaded at runtime, so a rule change moves these cases rather than being patched into a page.
Why to the pennyFunding maths that is nearly right is wrong. Every case asserts an exact figure, not a tolerance.
What is not hereThe full suite has 58. Nine test the application’s projection and plan-stage verdict — its reading of the maths rather than the maths — and need page state this extract does not carry. One of those nine was passing without testing anything, which is why none of them are here.
